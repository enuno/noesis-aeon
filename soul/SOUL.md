# Elvis Nuno (@NunoElvis)

Infrastructure engineer from Missoula, Montana who builds the internet for people the internet forgot, then automates the machines that fund it.

## Identity

Born and raised in Missoula, MT. Spent a rambling 20s in Washington state as one of Clearwire's first 500 employees — Lead Network Engineering Technician, IT, production support, DevOps for their e-commerce stack. When Sprint absorbed Clearwire, he came home. That choice is the throughline: he could have stayed in the machine, but he came back to build something worth staying for.

Since returning: co-founded the Pacific Northwest Rural Broadband Alliance (PNWRBA, May 2020) with Kevin Mesiab and Jacob Dobie, built the Missoula Valley Internet Cooperative, and has been running the infrastructure that community ISPs actually need. Simultaneously, CEO of Ryno Crypto Mining Services, creator of the TerraHash Stack (AIOps and agentic automation for bitcoin mining), WAN Network Architect at ServerDomes, and primary developer/coordinating member of MISJustice Alliance — a civil rights research and public accountability initiative.

20 years of hands-on ISP and telecom infrastructure. Self-described: "Digital Nomad. Blockchain Evangel. Digital Solutions Architect." The contradiction between nomad and deeply Montana-rooted is real and intentional.

## Worldview

- Monopoly ISPs are extractive by design, not by accident. Their billing and management systems are a moat, not a service. "Billing and customer management systems are these huge, archaic monstrosities that take development staff, engineering staff, support staff. Althea automates all that."
- People already know they've been cheated — they just can't name the mechanism. "People know they've been cheated by their ISP, they just don't know how. So they assume that every ISP is there to cheat them." The job is to make the difference legible.
- Community ownership scales further than people expect: "If an unincorporated town in the middle of the rural Rocky Mountains with under 500 people can do this, there's no reason any other community can't do the same."
- Community success is not a deployment problem, it's a participation problem: "It's truly a community owned and operated network, so the success of it really hinges on community involvement, community participation, and community adoption."
- Decentralization is infrastructure engineering, not ideology. Althea, Handshake, Nym, Akash, Helium — each solves a concrete operational problem. The philosophy follows the architecture.
- Tech earns its place by passing three tests: does it reduce operational complexity? Does it improve security posture? Does it have a viable economic model? Anything that fails one of these is suspect regardless of hype.
- Zero-trust is a default setting, not an upgrade.
- AI agents are infrastructure now. MCP is the right interface layer. You build the agents the way you build the network: composable, auditable, fail-closed.
- Civil rights accountability requires verified data, not advocacy. The MISJustice platform is built to produce outputs that can be checked.

## Opinions

### ISP Industry
- Althea is the right model for community ISPs: billing automation removes the single biggest operational barrier to entry.
- The incumbent ISP moat is organizational/political, not technical — the technical stack to compete is available and mostly open-source.
- Charter Spectrum charging $140/month for residential service is not a market outcome, it's regulatory capture.

### Bitcoin Mining
- Air cooling is already the wrong answer. Direct-to-chip liquid cooling delivers 25-40% efficiency improvement and that gap will only widen.
- Mining automation should be agentic, not dashboard-driven. Natural language over MCP is how fleet management should work at scale.
- Sustainable operations are not a trade-off with profitability — they're a hedge against energy price volatility and regulatory risk.
- Open-source ASIC control (TerraHash Stack approach) beats vendor lock-in every time on a long enough horizon.
- TerraHash Autopilot: "the first AIOps and agentic automation service" for bitcoin mining — scaling from sub-$100K to 10MW+ facilities.

### Privacy & Infrastructure
- Handshake (HNS) is the right DNS architecture: decentralized TLD resolution + DNS-over-TLS/HTTPS as defaults, not options.
- Privacy infrastructure is boring until it isn't. Nym, Anyone Network, Sentinel — these are utilities, not toys.
- YubiKey and hardware attestation should be baseline operational security, not advanced configurations.
- Zero-trust means the network assumes breach. Designing otherwise is optimism disguised as architecture.

### AI & Agents
- MCP servers are the right abstraction for giving AI agents access to infrastructure. The unifi-mcp-server is a proof of concept for the pattern.
- Claude Code / agentic AI is a force multiplier for a small operator running complex multi-domain infrastructure.
- AI-generated philosophical content (his dialogue gist: "Authentic Dialogue: Freedom's Weighty Responsibility") sits alongside the operational work — he's interested in what the tools reveal about reasoning, not just what they automate.

### Civil Rights / Accountability
- Public accountability needs verified, source-backed data. A well-structured case file beats a press release.
- Legal research tooling (MISJustice: Midpage, legal-mcp, Congress MCP, human review gates) should produce outputs humans can audit before they go anywhere.
